# Vicu 2.6.3

Vicu 2.6.3 brings comments to every task, opens images inside the app and adds roomier task rows. The AppImage now runs on Fedora Atomic. Update the phone app to 2.6.3 too, so both apps agree on comments and custom lists.

If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### New

- **Comments on every task.** Comments no longer need a shared project. Every open task has **Comments**, and on a project nobody else can see they are notes to yourself, with their count on the row. Assign, the unread mark, mentions and Notify me stay on shared projects. A custom list set to any comments now matches every project. ([#63](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu/issues/63))
- **Images open in the app.** An image attached to a task, or a picture in its notes, opens in a viewer over the app. It fits the window, a click shows it at its own size, Left and Right go through the task's images, and the header has Open in default app, Save and Close. Other files, HEIC and TIFF still open in the default app.
- **Comfortable task rows.** Settings has **Task rows**. Compact stays the default. Comfortable shows up to three lines of title and a line of notes.
- **Dates that say the day.** Date chips, on the open task and in the new task row among others, now give the word and the date for yesterday, today and tomorrow, such as "Today, 7 Oct".

### Fixes

- **Pictures in notes.** Opening a task could lose track of the pictures in its notes, so a later edit saved the notes without them. They now stay.
- **When popover.** Setting a date or a custom time no longer closes the When popover, whether the task moves up or down the list.
- **AppImage on Fedora Atomic.** The AppImage now uses the static runtime, which brings its own FUSE, so the system no longer needs FUSE 2. ([#62](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu/issues/62))
- **Menus.** Menu items that open a picker or a dialog, such as Schedule and Move to project, no longer end in dots.

### Issues closed

- [#62](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu/issues/62) AppImage does not open on Fedora Atomic out of the box
- [#63](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu/issues/63) Comments for single users

The phone app's issues closed by this release are listed in its [2.6.3 notes](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/releases/tag/v2.6.3).
