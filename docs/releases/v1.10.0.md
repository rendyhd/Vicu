# Vicu 1.10.0

Vicu 1.10.0 ships together with Vicu Android 1.10.0. The big change is a new look: both apps now share one design system, so colours, text, spacing, dates and motion match across desktop and Android. It also makes completing tasks calmer, picking dates faster, and syncing more reliable.

This is a pre-release that builds on 1.9.0; if you are coming from 1.8, read the [1.9.0 notes](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/releases/tag/v1.9.0) too. If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### A new look, shared with Android

- Vicu's own colours, with text that is easy to read in light and dark themes. Every label chip, date, button and mark is checked for readable contrast.
- One set of type sizes, spacing and corner radii in every window, including Quick Entry and Quick View.
- Each smart list has its own icon and colour in the sidebar.
- Task rows have a title line and a quieter line below with a project, labels and the date, and a bell and priority at the end. Sections have two levels of headers with counts, and a group of one gets no header.
- Priority is shown with bars (one to three for low, medium and high) and a filled square for urgent, with the name on hover and for screen readers. The same marks appear in rows, menus, Quick Entry and Quick View.
- Dates read the same way everywhere ("Today", "Tomorrow", weekdays, then dates) in your system's regional format. New **Clock** setting: System, 12-hour or 24-hour, for when your system uses a custom format.
- New task row at the end of each list. Add a section from the project menu or between sections.
- The Logbook is grouped by the day you completed tasks, with the time of day.
- Every view has the same page header, reading width and empty state, and lists show a header and placeholder rows while they load instead of "Loading...".
- The first-run setup starts with the logo and a welcome line, and shows every step in one card.
- Quick View rows have a round checkbox, the due date in words and the priority at the end, and follow the theme you chose in Settings.

### Completing tasks

- A completed task stays in the list for 5 seconds, longer while you point at it or it has keyboard focus, so you can still change your mind or finish what you were doing. Then it collapses.
- A single "Completed" message with **Undo** appears. Complete several in a row and it reads "3 completed". It stays while the pointer is over it.
- Undo of a repeating task restores its dates, not only the open state.
- With the keyboard, Space completes the task and moves to the next checkbox. Undo puts focus back.
- The checkbox pops, a check is drawn and the title is struck through.

### Picking a date: the When panel

One panel for every date and time:

- Type a date in your own words ("tomorrow 9am", "next friday") and watch the calendar follow.
- Quick choices, with the coming weekdays listed by name.
- A month calendar you can move through with the arrow keys, Home, End and Page Up and Down.
- Times, including a custom time that applies when you press Enter.

The panel opens inside the window and flips above its button when there is no room below.

### Quick find and the command palette

- **Quick find** sits at the top of the sidebar. Press **Ctrl+F** (**Command+F** on a Mac) and matching tasks appear as you type.
- A command palette opens with **Ctrl+Shift+P** (**Command+Shift+P** on a Mac), for jumping to a list or running a command without the mouse.
- Ctrl+K still completes the selected task and Ctrl+P still prints.

### Working with several tasks

- Select two or more tasks and a bar appears to schedule, complete, move, tag or delete them together.
- The task card shows the properties that are set as chips and the unset ones as quiet buttons.
- The context menu has one icon column, real shortcut hints, priority marks and a check by the current priority.
- Quick add suggests projects, labels and dates under the cursor as you type, and the chips above follow what you typed unless you set them yourself. This also works in Quick Entry.

### Projects

- The sidebar shows Areas with projects below them, collapsible, and a progress ring for each project that shows how much of it is done.
- Counts in the sidebar stay current after changes from Quick Entry, Quick View and other devices.

### Routines are optional

- Settings has a new **Routines** panel with two switches, both on by default.
- **Enable routines** turns the whole feature off: the sidebar entry, the Today section and routine reminders go away. Your routines and their history are kept, so switching it back on brings everything back.
- **Show routines in Today** controls the Today section on its own.
- Today lists only the routines still open for the day. Completed and skipped ones leave the list, the progress count still covers the whole day, and the section disappears when nothing is left. The Routines view still shows everything, so you can undo there.

### Motion

- A task opens into its card and closes back into its row.
- Changing views fades the content in and slides the sidebar highlight to the new item. Switching with the keyboard stays instant.
- Lists move smoothly: rows open and close, other rows slide into place when you reorder or open a card, and a task you drag lifts and settles into its slot.
- Menus, popovers and tooltips grow from the button that opened them.
- Counts roll when they change. When you empty Today it says "All clear" and offers the next task.
- When your system asks for reduced motion, all of this becomes a plain fade.

### Windows

- Native caption buttons, including **Snap Layouts** when you hover the maximise button.
- On Windows 11 22H2 and later the sidebar has the Mica material, so it picks up your wallpaper colour. Windows 10 keeps a solid sidebar.
- Text uses Segoe UI Variable.
- **Contrast themes** are supported: checkboxes, selection, focus, label chips and priority marks use your system colours, also in Quick Entry and Quick View.

### Keyboard and screen readers

- One clear focus ring everywhere, and click and touch targets of at least 24 pixels.
- Menus, dialogs and pickers are real menus, dialogs and listboxes: arrow keys, typing to jump to an item, Escape to close, and focus returns to where you were.
- Task rows form a list with arrow-key navigation, and every checkbox, toggle and button in Settings has a name.
- Completions, deletions and toasts are announced politely, and each toast type has an icon and a name.
- Task info is a proper dialog, and sections of Notifications that are switched off cannot be tabbed into.

### Sync that holds up

- Changes are sent to the server one at a time. Bulk actions on many tasks no longer lose changes on Vikunja servers that use SQLite ("database is locked").
- A stopped sync now retries after 5 seconds, then 15 seconds, then every minute, instead of waiting for the next change.

### Fixes

- Review uses clearer states: "Not reviewed yet" is grey and "Due" is amber.
- A toast that is removed early no longer leaves a completed task stuck in the list.
- Deleting a task moves focus to a sensible place.
- Clearing the date of several tasks at once works.
- The reminder panel keeps keyboard focus while you set a reminder.

### Under the hood

- Colours, type sizes, corner radii and motion come from one token file shared byte for byte with Vicu Android, and tests check every colour pair for readable contrast in light and dark.
- A Tailwind quirk that silently dropped tinted backgrounds (selection, hover and date chips) is fixed at the source, with a test that keeps it from coming back.
- A new UI verification harness drives the built app against a local Vikunja with real mouse and keyboard input, takes screenshots, runs accessibility checks and measures frame timing.

### Upgrade notes

- Update both apps to 1.10.0 to get the same look and the same date wording on both.
- Android now uses Vicu colours by default. Android's **Use device colours** switch is still available in Settings.
- To see animations in Vicu on Windows, turn on Settings, Accessibility, Visual effects, Animation effects. When it is off, Vicu follows it and keeps the quiet fades.
