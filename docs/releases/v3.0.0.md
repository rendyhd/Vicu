# Vicu 3.0.0

Vicu 3.0.0 brings two big additions: your calendar on the Plan screen, and Vicu Pro, an optional
set of tools for people who lead work with others. Pro is free, built in and off until you turn it
on. With Pro off, Vicu works exactly as before. Update the phone app to 3.0.0 too, so both apps
read events, Pro's data and the new list conditions the same way.

If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### Events and calendars

- **Your events on the Plan screen.** Add your own events (one-off or repeating) and see them next
  to your time blocks. Move, resize and change them like blocks; a repeating event asks whether the
  change is for this time, this and the following, or all.
- **Connected calendars.** Show a calendar link (ICS), a CalDAV account, a Microsoft 365 or Google
  account, or, on a Mac, the calendars of System Settings. Calendars are read only and stay on this
  device. Busy only shows a block of busy time without titles.
- **Plan my day** counts events as busy time.
- **Import a calendar file** into your own events, with one Undo.
- **Plan's settings moved** to Settings, Pro, next to Pro's tools. Plan works the same whether Pro
  is on or off.

### Vicu Pro

Turn it on in Settings, Pro, and choose the parts you want. [What Pro does](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/blob/main/docs/pro.md).

- **Know what you can do now.** Lists can show only what is available now, and each row says why
  a task is not: it starts later, waits on another task, is not the next step of a sequential
  project, or is on hold. Start dates (`starts monday`), After, flags (`!flag`, Mod+Shift+F),
  estimates (`~30m`), project types and statuses, Focus (Mod+Shift+O), and repeats that end.
- **Promises and people.** Mark a label as a person, then `Jan: send the deck friday` is Jan's
  promise to you and `for Jan: ...` yours to Jan. Give verdicts when they are due (keys 1 to 6),
  see what others owe you in Waiting on, and open a person for their promises, the last 90 days in
  plain words and a 1:1 agenda. Copy a recap. Import what Microsoft 365 Copilot or another assistant
  found in your meeting notes or mail (Mod+Shift+M); nothing is added until you choose Add.
- **Portfolio.** Initiatives with a cap, a rank and parking; readiness; escalations
  drafted when a promise is missed twice; the Sponsor desk with a one-pager to copy or print;
  request triage (`req:`); and meetings that go through the people one card at a time.
- **Power tools.** Custom lists gain Pro conditions, grouping, the fields rows show and
  starter lists; lists can show as a sortable table; the inspector (Mod+I) sets start, estimate and
  flag for a selection; Plan my day uses estimates and proposes a daily focus block; the weekly
  review gains the execution steps and a mirror of your week; and a quarterly impact summary.
- **Pro's assistant** (off by default): Ask can look up promises, people and the portfolio, the
  import converts plain notes, and a meeting card drafts promises from what was said.
- **`vicu://` links** open a task, a view or Quick Entry from other apps and scripts.

Pro keeps its records in one hidden task in your Inbox on your own server, like Vicu's other
synced data. Turning Pro off writes nothing; turning it on again finds everything where it was.
