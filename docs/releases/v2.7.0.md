# Vicu 2.7.0

Vicu 2.7.0 adds a day planner, task templates and filters in Search. Comments can hold files, and text finally has a right-click menu. Update the phone app to 2.7.0 too, so both apps agree on time blocks, templates and subtask order.

If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### New

- **Plan your day.** Plan puts a list of tasks beside a timeline. Pick a list, drag a task onto the timeline or draw on empty time, and look at a day, three days or a week. Move a block or either edge by pointer or keyboard. Routines and due times show as pills at their times. Fit gives a day's tasks without a time the next free slots, with Undo. New task (Ctrl+N, File, or the palette) opens a title box at the day's next free time. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, with Show Plan in the sidebar. [How it works](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/blob/v2.7.0/docs/planner.md). The overlap columns, gestures and drop come from the day calendar [@chuwy](https://github.com/chuwy) proposed in [#58](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/pull/58), with thanks; they now sit on the task's own start and end dates, so the phone reads them too. ([#53](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues/53), [#60](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues/60))
- **Plan my day.** A short session that takes you through leftovers, choosing what to do and arranging it. Nothing is placed until you accept, and Undo all puts it back. It can remind you when each block starts. With the assistant set up it suggests times and lengths, and it works fully without. An evening notification can offer it for tomorrow.
- **Time blocks from quick add.** "Call Sam tomorrow at 3pm for 30m" makes a task with that time set aside, from the new task row, Quick Entry, pasted lines and voice, whether the planner is on or not. A block shows on its row, in Quick View and in Ask's answers, and puts the task on Today on that day. It moves with the due date, a duplicate keeps it, and a new task's default reminder goes by the block's start. Standalone mode has no blocks, because a local task only keeps a due date.
- **Task templates.** Save as template, in a task's More menu and context menu, keeps its project, notes, labels, priority, repeat, due date as days from today, reminders that count from the due date, and subtasks. The new task row then has a last Template button that lists your templates, with a small arrow to edit or remove one. Templates reach your other devices.
- **Filters in Search.** Chips under the title narrow by date, status, labels and priority, for as long as the page is open, with or without text.
- **Files in comments.** Attach a file with the paperclip, a paste or a drop. Images show in the thread and open in the viewer, and other files offer Open and Save. An edit keeps the files, and deleting a comment leaves them on the task, which the question says. A file deleted from the task reads "No longer available".
- **Image thumbnails.** An image in a task's attachments shows a preview, and the preview and the name both open the viewer.
- **Right-click menu for text.** Spelling suggestions, Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All in a text field, and Copy on selected text anywhere else. Menus of rows and the sidebar are unchanged.
- **Move subtasks.** A grip appears on hover, and a subtask's menu has Move up and Move down. The order is kept for your account, so other people and Vikunja's web app keep the server's order.
- **Change a date in Ask.** A due date on a proposal card can be changed before you apply it, and the assistant is told what you changed.
- **Long notes.** Long notes fold at half the visible height with **Show all**. List lines have Move line up and down in the text menu and with Alt+Up and Alt+Down.

### Fixes

- **Dragging a task.** A task dropped on a project, label, section or Plan now lands on its target instead of flying back to its old place first. While you drag, the cursor stays a grabbing hand, rows show no hover or tooltip, and the task is a small chip beside the pointer so the target stays visible.
- **Words in a title.** An amount of time is only a date right after "in". "Call 30m", "Rest 3 days" and "Plan the week" keep their words in the title instead of turning them into a date.
- **Mentions.** A key pressed right after picking a mention stays where it was typed, so "see you" no longer comes out as "ee yous".
- **New task from the palette.** The new task row could close again right after opening when the list was still loading.
- **Refused uploads.** A file the server refuses now counts as failed in the file dialog.
- **Comment buttons.** Edit and Delete on a comment are larger targets.
- **Task card.** The checkbox lines up with the title, and the subtask field stays in view while you add several in a row.
