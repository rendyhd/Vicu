## In short

- **Pro lists**: custom lists can pick tasks by availability, flag, estimate, project status, promise and
  person, group them, and choose what each row shows. Four starter lists get you going.
- **The table**: show a custom list as a table and sort by any column.
- **The inspector**: set the start date, estimate and flag of many tasks at once.
- **Plan my day** uses your estimates and proposes a **focus block** for your top initiatives.
- **The weekly review** gets execution steps and a **mirror** line; **Portfolio** gets a **quarterly
  impact** summary.

> [!PRO]
> This page is part of [Vicu Pro](guide:pro) and works on the desktop. Turn on Pro and the part **Power
> tools** in **Settings, Pro**. {desktop}

## Make a Pro list {desktop}

Custom lists are the place for views of your own (see [Custom lists](guide:custom-lists)). With Power
tools on, the list editor has a **Pro** section:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Show** | **Every task**, **Available now**, **Remaining (open)** |
| **Flagged** | **Any**, **Flagged**, **Not flagged** |
| **Estimate at most (minutes)** | Only tasks estimated at this length or less |
| **Project status** | **Any**, **Active**, **On hold** |
| **Promise** | **Any task**, **Promised to me**, **I promised**, **Either** |
| **People** | Only tasks for these people |
| **Group by** | **No groups**, **Project**, **Label**, **Person**, **Due date**, **Start date**, **Flagged** |
| **Rows show** | Which details each row shows: **Project**, **Labels**, **Due date**, **Start date**, **Estimate**, **Flag**, **Notes** |

The editor counts the open tasks that match as you change the settings.

### Start from a ready-made list

On a new list, **Start from** offers four lists you can use as they are or change:

| Starter | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Next actions** | Every available task, grouped by project |
| **Waiting on** | Promises others made you, grouped by person, by due date |
| **Quick wins** | Available tasks of 15 minutes or less, with project, due date and estimate |
| **Flagged by project** | Flagged tasks, grouped by project |

> [!NOTE]
> A list's Pro conditions are kept and followed on every device, Pro on or off, and on the phone too. A
> list made for "Quick wins" never suddenly shows everything somewhere else. The editor keeps showing the
> Pro section for such a list even where Power tools are off, so you can change it.

## See a list as a table {desktop}

At the top of a custom list, switch between **Rows** and **Table**.

- Click a column's header to sort by it; click again to reverse.
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click a second column to sort by it within the first.
- Rows select like list rows: click, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> on a
  Mac), and open with a double click or <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Change many tasks at once {desktop}

The inspector sets Pro's fields of several tasks in one go.

1. Select two or more tasks.
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd>, or choose **Inspector** in the selection bar.
3. Set **Start**, **Estimate** or **Flag** (**Flag all** or **Unflag**). A field that differs between
   the tasks says **Mixed**; **Clear** removes it from all of them.

## Plan my day with estimates and a focus block {desktop}

[Plan my day](guide:plan) lays your day out as time blocks. With Power tools on:

- A task's **estimate** is its length in the plan, so the meter shows how full your day really is.
- The plan starts with a **focus block** of 60 to 90 minutes "for the work that moves your top
  initiatives": the available tasks of your highest-ranked [initiatives](guide:pro/portfolio). Keep it,
  or untick its tasks like any other.

## The weekly review, for execution {desktop}

The [weekly review](guide:review) gets five steps before its usual ones, each with a count:

| Step | What it is about |
|---|---|
| **Give verdicts** | Promises others made you that are due: kept, not yet, or another verdict |
| **Send what was agreed** | What was agreed in a meeting and not sent in a recap yet |
| **Escalations and decisions** | Escalations drafted or on the agenda, and decisions you are owed |
| **Check the portfolio** | Initiatives not ready, over the cap or without an available task |
| **Note the value delivered** | Initiatives closed this week without the value they delivered |

Each step has **Open** to go there and **Mark done**.

**The mirror** is one line about your last seven days, for example: "Last 7 days: you took over 1 promise
and had a focus block on 3 of 5 workdays. You hold 42 open tasks; others hold 6 promises to you." It is a
reflection, not a score.

## Look back on a quarter {desktop}

On **Portfolio**, choose **Quarterly impact** for a summary of **This quarter** or **Last quarter**: the
value delivered, the initiatives closed, the decisions made and how promises went. Add a **Growth** note
about your own quarter; it appears only in the copy. Copy it for a performance review or a report, or print
it.
