## In short

- **People** are labels you mark as a person. Nothing new to set up: the labels you already use for
  people work.
- **A promise** is a task with a direction: someone promised it to you, or you promised it to them. Type
  `Jan: send the deck friday` or `for Jan: review the budget`.
- **When a promise is due**, Vicu asks for a **verdict**: Delivered, Late, Not yet, New date or Dropped.
  Promises due are at the top of Today.
- **Waiting on** lists what others owe you; **the person page** has everything with one person and the
  agenda for your next 1:1.
- **Recaps** turn what was agreed into text for mail or chat. On the desktop you can also run
  **meetings** and **import** notes from Microsoft 365 Copilot or another assistant. {desktop}

> [!PRO]
> This page is part of [Vicu Pro](guide:pro). Turn on Pro and the part **Promises and people** in
> **Settings, Pro** on the desktop or **Settings, General, Pro** on the phone.

## Mark a label as a person {desktop} {phone}

1. Right-click the label in the sidebar (on the phone, open the label's menu).
2. Choose **This is a person**.

The person now appears under **People** in the sidebar, with how many open promises go each way. To
undo it, choose **Not a person**; the label stays a normal label.

A person can go by other names. When an import meets a name it does not know, it asks **Who is this?**,
and the name you match is remembered for that person, so "Jan" and "Jan de Vries" end up in the same
place.

## Note a promise {desktop} {phone}

The quickest way is the title, with the person's name and a colon at the very start:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `Jan: send the deck friday` | Jan's promise to you, due Friday |
| `for Jan: review the budget` | Your promise to Jan |
| `Priya de Boer: share the numbers tomorrow` | Priya's promise, due tomorrow |

The name must be a person Vicu knows (a label marked as a person, one of their other names, or a first
name only one person has). An unknown name stays in the title. The person's label is added to the task.

You can also turn any task into a promise: open it and use **Promise**. Choose **They promised me** or
**I promised them**, the person, and optionally:

- **In their words** or **What you said**: the promise as it was said, so nobody argues later about
  what was meant.
- **We will see it is done when**: what done looks like.

A promise someone made you shows the person's mark in place of the checkbox, because it is not yours to
tick. Your own promises keep their checkbox. A promise someone made you never counts as available, so
it stays out of your Available lists (see [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available)).

> [!TIP]
> If you set a **Work area** in Pro's settings, only promises in projects of those categories count. Use
> it to keep promises at home out of your work views.

## Give a verdict {desktop} {phone}

When a promise someone made you is due, Vicu asks what happened. Promises due today or earlier are at
the top of **Today**, and each has the verdict choices:

| Key | Verdict | Means |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Delivered** | Done, on time |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **Late** | Done, but late |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Not yet** | Not done. Pick when to **Ask again on**, and optionally **What is in the way?** |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **New date** | They asked for more time. Pick the new date |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | **Dropped** | It will not happen |
| <kbd>6</kbd> | **Ask me tomorrow** | You do not know yet |

On the desktop, focus the row and press the number, or click the choice. On the phone, tap the person's
mark on the row. Every verdict can be undone from the message that follows.

If the person completed the task themselves (in a shared project, for example), Vicu asks once:
**Delivered by** them, or **I did it**.

A missed date counts. When a person misses the same promise twice, and you use the
[Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio), Vicu drafts an escalation for you to look at.

### Your own promises

Your promises to others are tasks like any other: complete them when they are done. They are listed on
the person's page under **You owe** them, so you see them before you meet.

## See what others owe you {desktop} {phone}

**Waiting on** in the sidebar lists the open promises others made you, grouped by person, the soonest
first. Tasks with a label on hold (for example "waiting for budget") are listed too.

## Prepare a 1:1 on the person page {desktop} {phone}

Click a person under **People** to open their page:

- **Next 1:1**: an agenda made from what is open between you: "What's still unclear?", their promises
  that are due or missed, what you owe them, and decisions they owe.
- **From** them: what they promised you.
- **You owe** them: what you promised.
- **To confirm**: tasks named "Confirm with" them, made by an import or a triage when something they
  said was not a clear yes. Check it with them and complete the task.
- A line about the **last 90 days**, in plain words, for example "Kept 5 of 6 on time in the last 90
  days, 1 late." It is never a score.

On the desktop, **Copy agenda** puts the agenda on the clipboard for a meeting invite or chat. On the
phone, share the agenda instead.

## Send a recap {desktop} {phone}

After a conversation, send what was agreed so everyone has the same list.

1. Open **Recap** on a person's page, from the command palette, or after an import or a meeting.
2. Choose the people and the days to include (on the desktop, **From** a date; on the phone, the last
   1, 7 or 30 days).
3. Copy it (desktop) or share it (phone), and paste it into mail or chat.

The promises in a copied recap are marked as sent, which the [weekly review](guide:pro/power) uses to
remind you of agreements nobody sent yet.

## Run a meeting {desktop}

For a team meeting or a round of 1:1s, a meeting goes through the people one at a time.

1. On **People**, choose **New meeting**.
2. Give it a name, add the **People, in round order** (use **Earlier** and **Later** to change the
   order), and optionally a rhythm: weekly, every two weeks or monthly.
3. Save. The meeting is listed under **Meetings** on People.

To run it, open the meeting and choose **Start round**. Each person gets a card with their open
promises. Give verdicts as you go (keys <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>6</kbd> on a promise) and add new promises
on the card. **Your card: what you owe the room** holds your own; type in **You promise...** and choose
**Add**.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Next person |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Skip this person |
| <kbd>A</kbd> | Mark this person absent |

There is no timer, and you can take the people in any order. Everything is written as it happens.
**End meeting** writes the round (who was discussed, skipped and absent) and opens its recap.

## Import notes from Copilot or another assistant {desktop}

If your meetings are summarized by Microsoft 365 Copilot (or another assistant), Vicu can read what it
found: promises, your actions, decisions and open questions.

:::steps
1. Choose **Ask Copilot** on a person's page or in the command palette. Pick what the notes are (this
   meeting, this mail thread or this chat) and the date, keep **Open promises** ticked so Copilot can
   report on them, and choose **Copy prompt**.
2. In Copilot, paste the prompt next to your meeting, mail or chat and run it.
3. Copy Copilot's whole answer.
4. In Vicu, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> on a Mac), or simply
   paste it into a list or into Quick Entry. The **Import** window opens.
5. Check the list. Every item says what it will do: a new promise, your action, an update to an open
   promise, a decision, a value note. Fix a person with **Who is this?** or leave an item out.
6. Choose **Add**. Nothing is added before that.
:::

After the import, **Recap** sends the people involved what was agreed.

With **Assistant help in Pro** on, Import can also read plain notes that are not in the prompt's format
(**Convert with the assistant**), and a meeting card can draft promises from what was said. See
[the assistant](guide:assistant).

## Details

- **What a promise is stored as.** The task is a normal Vikunja task with the person's label. Its
  direction, words, verdicts and history are kept in Pro's hidden record on your server, which every
  device with Pro reads.
- **Turning this part off** hides the person marks, verdicts and screens. The labels stay labels and
  the promise tasks stay tasks.
- **On the phone.** {phone} People, Waiting on, the person page, promises due on Today, verdicts, recaps
  and capture of promise words are on the phone. When you note a promise there, it asks for **In their
  words** or **What you said** and when you will see it is done. Meetings and import are on the desktop.
