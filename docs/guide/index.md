## Vicu Pro

Vicu stays simple for everyone. **Vicu Pro** is an optional switch for more complex work: what you can
do right now, promises people made you, initiatives you steer, and faster ways through many tasks.
Pro only adds; with it off, Vicu is the app you know.

:::cards
- [What Pro is](guide:pro): Who it is for, its four parts, and how to try it safely.

## About Vicu

Vicu is a free, open-source task manager for [Vikunja](https://vikunja.io/), the open-source to-do
server you host yourself. Your tasks live on your server, not with Vicu. Vicu is an independent app, not
made by the Vikunja team. Found something that is wrong or missing here? Each page has a link at the
bottom to suggest a change, or [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu/issues).
