## In short

- Terms are in alphabetical order. Terms that belong to [Vicu Pro](guide:pro) are marked {pro}.
- Missing a word? [Open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu/issues) and we will add it.

## A

- **After** {pro}: A way to say a task waits on other tasks. The row reads "After: ..." and the task is not available until they are done. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).
- **Anytime**: The smart list of every open task outside the Inbox, with or without a date. See [Smart lists](guide:lists).
- **Area**: A top-level project that holds other projects below it. The sidebar and the phone's drawer show it as a collapsible group. See [Projects and labels](guide:projects).
- **Ask**: The assistant's chat. You ask about your tasks, and it answers briefly and proposes changes as cards you apply or decline. See [The assistant](guide:assistant).
- **Assistant**: An optional helper that uses an AI model you choose. It turns what you say or paste into tasks and answers questions. Off until you set a provider. See [The assistant](guide:assistant).
- **Available** {pro}: A task you can do right now: not waiting for a start date, another task, or an on-hold project. Lists can show **Available**, **Remaining** or **All**. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).

## B

- **Block**: See time block.
- **Bottom bar** {phone}: The row of lists at the bottom of the phone app. The Inbox is always first and you pick the other three. See [On your phone](guide:phone).
- **Brain dump**: Talk or type everything on your mind, and the assistant organizes it into a plan by project, with new projects where none fit. Nothing is added until you accept. See [The assistant](guide:assistant).

## C

- **Cap** {pro}: The most initiatives you keep active at once, four unless you agree otherwise. Adding one more asks you to park or finish one. See [Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio).
- **Category**: An optional named header that groups your top-level projects in the sidebar or drawer. Categories sync to all your devices. See [Projects and labels](guide:projects).
- **Command palette** {desktop}: A search box that finds lists, projects, labels and tasks and runs actions. Open it with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>. See [On the desktop](guide:desktop).
- **Completion hold**: A completed task stays in the list for about 5 seconds, shown as done, before it collapses, so you can undo it. See [Work with tasks](guide:tasks).
- **Custom list**: A list of your own made from conditions such as project, label, due window and priority. It is stored on your server, so every device shows it. See [Custom lists](guide:custom-lists).

## D

- **Default reminder**: A reminder added automatically to every new task that has a due date and no reminder of its own. See [Settings](guide:settings).
- **Drawer** {phone}: The side menu on the phone with your lists, projects and labels. It is the phone's version of the sidebar. See [On your phone](guide:phone).

## E

- **Escalation** {pro}: A draft message for your sponsor, made when a promise is missed twice (or the number you set). You can put it on an agenda, copy it, resolve it or dismiss it. See [Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio).
- **Estimate** {pro}: How long a task takes, typed as `~30m`. Plan my day uses it as the task's length. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).
- **Event**: Something on your calendar, as opposed to a task. Your own Events sync between devices and appear on the Plan screen as busy time. Calendars you connect are shown the same way. See [Events and calendars](guide:calendars).

## F

- **Fit**: A Plan action that gives tasks without a time the next free slots in your day. See [Plan your day](guide:plan).
- **Flag** {pro}: Marks a task for attention. It is Vikunja's favorite star, so other apps see it. Typing `!flag` sets it. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).
- **Focus** {pro}: Shows only one area of your projects everywhere until you leave it. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).

## G

- **Guided session**: A review the app leads one decision at a time: Sort the Inbox, Sort out overdue, Waiting a long time, and Review projects. See [Review and guided organizing](guide:review).

## H

- **Hidden task**: A task Vicu marks done and hides everywhere in Vicu. It holds data such as custom lists, routines, synced settings and Pro records. You may see it in other Vikunja apps. Do not delete it. See [Sync and offline](guide:sync#the-hidden-tasks).

## I

- **Inbox**: Where new tasks land when you add them without choosing a project. You pick which project is the Inbox, and that choice syncs. See [How Vicu is organized](guide:basics).
- **Initiative** {pro}: A project you steer as a portfolio item, with a rank, a readiness check and a sponsor. Start one from the project's menu. See [Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio).

## L

- **Label**: A colored tag you put on tasks. Each label has its own page. See [Projects and labels](guide:projects).
- **Logbook**: The smart list of finished tasks, grouped by the day you completed them. See [Smart lists](guide:lists).

## O

- **Offline queue**: The list of changes made without a connection. They wait and go to the server in order when you are back. See [Sync and offline](guide:sync#the-offline-queue).
- **On hold** {pro}: A project or label that Pro treats as paused, so its tasks are not available. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).

## P

- **Parked** {pro}: An initiative you set aside with a reason. Its tasks leave Today and Upcoming until you resume it. See [Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio).
- **Person** {pro}: A label you marked as a person with **This is a person**. People can owe you promises, and you can owe them. See [Promises and people](guide:pro/promises).
- **Plan**: The optional screen with a timeline, where you give tasks a time. Off until you turn it on. See [Plan your day](guide:plan).
- **Plan my day**: A four-step session that takes you from leftover tasks to a planned day: Leftovers, Choose, Arrange and Ready. See [Plan your day](guide:plan).
- **Project**: A group of tasks. Projects can contain subprojects, be split into sections, and be shared with others. See [Projects and labels](guide:projects).
- **Project review**: Stepping through your projects on a schedule to keep them current. Each shows when it was last reviewed. See [Review and guided organizing](guide:review).
- **Promise** {pro}: Something a person committed to, or that you committed to them. It has a due date and ends with a verdict. See [Promises and people](guide:pro/promises).

## Q

- **Quick add**: Typing a task's title with dates, labels, projects and priority in plain words. See [Quick add syntax](guide:quick-add).
- **Quick Entry** {desktop}: A small box that opens over any app from a global shortcut so you can add a task fast. See [On the desktop](guide:desktop).
- **Quick find** {desktop}: The search box at the top of the sidebar that matches tasks as you type. See [On the desktop](guide:desktop).
- **Quick View** {desktop}: A floating list from a global shortcut where you check off tasks without switching windows. See [On the desktop](guide:desktop).

## R

- **Readiness** {pro}: A nine-item checklist on an initiative: sponsor, owner, why, what done looks like, and more. Adding a task to an unready initiative asks first. See [Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio).
- **Recap** {pro}: A text summary of what was agreed with someone, to copy or share. See [Promises and people](guide:pro/promises).
- **Review**: The list that shows which projects are due for a check. See also project review and weekly review. See [Review and guided organizing](guide:review).
- **Routine**: A habit that comes back on a schedule, with up to five times a day. A missed one is "Not logged", never overdue. See [Routines](guide:routines).

## S

- **Saved filter**: A filter you saved in Vikunja. Vicu can import it as a custom list. See [Custom lists](guide:custom-lists).
- **Section**: A collapsible group of tasks inside a project, with its own header and count. See [Projects and labels](guide:projects).
- **Smart dictation**: Voice input where you keep talking and the assistant turns each pause into draft task cards. See [The assistant](guide:assistant).
- **Smart list**: One of Vicu's built-in lists: Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Anytime and Logbook. See [Smart lists](guide:lists).
- **Sponsor desk** {pro}: A desktop screen with escalations, decisions you are owed, initiatives without agreed capacity, and a one-page summary. See [Portfolio](guide:pro/portfolio).
- **Standalone mode** {desktop}: Tasks kept only on the computer, without a Vikunja server, as earlier versions allowed. No Plan, time blocks or Pro. See [Sync and offline](guide:sync#standalone-mode).
- **Start date** {pro}: The date a task can begin. Until then it is not available. It is Vikunja's own start date. See [Know what you can do now](guide:pro/available).
- **Subtask**: A task inside another task, shown in the parent's card or under it. See [Work with tasks](guide:tasks).
- **Synced setting**: A setting stored on your server so it follows you to every device. See [Settings](guide:settings).

## T

- **Task template**: A saved task, with its project, notes, labels, dates counted in days from today, reminders and subtasks. Use it to start a new task. See [Work with tasks](guide:tasks).
- **Time block**: Time you set aside for a task, with a start and an end on its due day. Type `at 3pm for 30m` to make one. It shows on Today and on Plan. See [Plan your day](guide:plan).
- **Today**: The smart list of what is due before the start of tomorrow: an Overdue section, today's tasks, tasks whose time block starts today, and today's routines. See [Smart lists](guide:lists).

## U

- **Upcoming**: The smart list of everything from tomorrow on, grouped by day. See [Smart lists](guide:lists).

## V

- **Verdict** {pro}: Your judgment on a promise when it is due: Delivered, Late, Not yet, New date, Dropped or Ask me tomorrow. See [Promises and people](guide:pro/promises).

## W

- **Waiting on** {pro}: A view of what others owe you, by person. See [Promises and people](guide:pro/promises).
- **Weekly review**: A checklist for the end of the week, from sorting the Inbox to a look ahead. Finishing it records the date. See [Review and guided organizing](guide:review).
- **When panel**: The one panel for setting a date and time on a task: type it in words, pick a quick choice, or use the calendar. See [Work with tasks](guide:tasks).
- **Work hours**: The start and end of your day on Plan. Free time, Fit and Plan my day use them. See [Settings](guide:settings).
