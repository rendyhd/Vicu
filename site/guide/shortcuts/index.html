    Keyboard shortcuts

    Every keyboard shortcut in the desktop app, grouped by where you use it. The phone apps have no keyboard shortcuts of their own.

    #In short

    • Keys are written for Windows and Linux. On a Mac, use Cmd where this page says Ctrl, and Option where it says Alt.
    • Three shortcuts can be changed: Quick Entry, Quick View and the voice shortcut, in Settings. The rest are fixed.
    • The two shortcuts that work in any app are Quick Entry and Quick View, after you turn them on.
    • A shortcut that does nothing is usually in the wrong place. For example, the list shortcuts need the list to have focus, and the Pro shortcuts need Pro to be on.
    • Settings, Keyboard Shortcuts shows the main shortcuts inside the app.

    #Shortcuts you can change Desktop

    ShortcutDefaultWhere to change it
    Quick EntryAlt+Shift+V, or Cmd+Shift+Space on a MacSettings, Quick Entry / View. Click the hotkey and press the new one.
    Quick ViewAlt+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on a MacSettings, Quick Entry / View
    Voice inputNone. It stays off until you record one.Settings, Keyboard Shortcuts, Voice input. It works only while Vicu is in front.

    Both Quick shortcuts work from any app and must be turned on first. See On the desktop.

    #Everywhere in the app

    KeysWhat it does
    Ctrl+Shift+POpen the command palette
    Ctrl+FGo to Quick find
    Ctrl+NNew task (File, New task)
    Ctrl+JShow or hide Ask
    Ctrl+Shift+CShow or hide comments
    Ctrl+PPrint the list (File, Print)
    F5 or Ctrl+RRefresh from the server (View, Refresh)
    Ctrl+Shift+RReload the window (View, Reload window)
    Cmd+,Open Settings (macOS app menu)

    The View menu also has zoom in, zoom out, reset zoom and full screen.

    #Moving through a list

    KeysWhat it does
    Up and DownPrevious and next task
    EnterOpen the task
    EscClose the task, clear focus, or clear the selection
    SpaceComplete the focused task and move to the next
    Ctrl+ASelect all tasks
    Delete or BackspaceDelete the selected tasks. It asks first if Confirm before deleting is on.
    Alt+Shift+Up or DownMove the task up or down (a manual order, or among tasks without a date)

    #Working on tasks

    KeysWhat it does
    Ctrl+KComplete the selected task. In an open task, complete and close it.
    Ctrl+TSet the due date to today
    Ctrl+DDuplicate the selected tasks
    Ctrl+CCopy the selected tasks' titles
    Ctrl+VMake tasks from the clipboard, one task per line
    !Due today. Type it in a title, or on a list.

    #In an open task

    KeysWhat it does
    Enter (in the title)Move to the notes
    EscSave and close
    Ctrl+EnterSave and close
    Ctrl+KComplete and close
    Ctrl+Shift+CShow or hide comments
    Ctrl+Shift+V (in notes)Paste without turning Markdown into formatting
    Alt+Up or Down (in a notes list)Move the line up or down
    Left and Right (image viewer)Previous and next image

    #Projects in the sidebar

    KeysWhat it does
    F2Rename the focused project
    Shift+F10 or the Menu keyOpen its menu
    Alt+Shift+arrow keysMove the project up or down, into the one above, or out one level

    #Plan

    KeysWhat it does
    Ctrl+NNew task at the day's next free time
    Ctrl+Shift+NNew event
    Alt+Shift+Up or DownMove a block or your event 15 minutes earlier or later
    Shift+Up or DownMake it 15 minutes shorter or longer
    EnterOpen the block's lengths

    See Plan your day.

    #Ask and comments

    KeysWhat it does
    Ctrl+JShow or hide Ask
    Enter (in Ask)Send the message
    Ctrl+Enter (in the text box for making tasks)Make tasks from the text
    Ctrl+Enter (in comments)Post the comment
    @ (in comments)Mention someone
    Esc (in comments)Close comments

    See The assistant and Work with others.

    #Guided sessions

    In a card session such as Sort the Inbox:

    KeysWhat it does
    Enter or RightNext. Saves the card's choices.
    LeftBack
    SSkip
    1 to 4The quick dates: Today, Tomorrow, This weekend, Next week
    DPick a date
    0No date
    PPick a project
    E or F2Edit the title
    CComplete
    DeleteDelete
    EscLeave the session

    On a project review page, R marks the project reviewed and S skips it. See Review and guided organizing.

    #Quick Entry and Quick View

    These keys work inside their small windows.

    KeysWhat it does
    Enter (Quick Entry)Save the task
    Tab (Quick Entry)Add a description
    Ctrl+L (Quick Entry)Link the browser tab or Obsidian note
    Ctrl+Left or Right (Quick Entry)Switch project. The modifier is a setting.
    Up, Down, Enter (Quick View)Move, and complete the selected task
    Tab, Shift+Enter, ! (Quick View)Show the description, edit, set due today
    EscClose the window

    #Pro Pro

    These work only while Pro is on.

    KeysWhat it does
    Ctrl+Shift+FFlag or unflag the selected tasks
    Ctrl+Shift+OFocus on some projects
    Ctrl+Shift+MImport meeting notes
    Ctrl+IOpen the inspector for two or more selected tasks
    1 to 6 on a promise rowDelivered, Late, Not yet, New date, Dropped, Ask me tomorrow
    Enter, S, A in a meeting roundNext person, skip, absent

    Ctrl+Shift+I stays the developer tools. See Promises and people and Power tools.
