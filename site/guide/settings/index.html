    Settings

    A reference to each settings section on the desktop and on the phone. Every table says which settings are synced through your server and which stay on the device where you set them.

    On this page
    • In short
    • Desktop
    • Phone
    • Accessibility

    #In short

    • Open Settings from the sidebar (desktop) or the drawer (phone). The desktop has tabs: General, Projects, Quick Entry / View, Notifications, Assistant, Pro and Keyboard Shortcuts. The phone has General, Projects, Notifications, Gestures and Assistant.
    • Most settings are per device. Look, behavior, notification and shortcut choices stay on the device where you set them. Setting the theme on your phone does not change your computer.
    • Some settings are synced. They live on your server and appear on every device you sign in on: your Inbox, task order, custom lists, categories, templates, planner defaults, and a few more. Tables below mark them Synced.
    • Settings save as you change them. There is no Save button.
    • Only a few shortcuts can be changed. See Keyboard shortcuts.

    How to read the tables: Synced follows you to every device. This device stays where you set it.

    #Accessibility

    Vicu follows your system's accessibility settings; there is nothing to turn on in Vicu.

    • Keyboard Desktop: everything works from the keyboard. Lists move with the arrow keys, menus and pickers jump to an item as you type its name, and one focus ring shows where you are. See Keyboard shortcuts.
    • Screen readers All devices: menus, dialogs and lists are real controls with names, and changes such as "Completed" are announced. On the phone, every task row has screen reader actions, such as Complete, Due today and Due tomorrow.
    • Less motion All devices: when your system asks for reduced motion, Vicu replaces movement with simple fades. On Windows that is Settings, Accessibility, Visual effects, Animation effects.
    • Contrast themes Desktop: with a Windows contrast theme, Vicu uses the system's colors and keeps every meaning visible by shape or text, not color alone. Priority, for example, is shown by bars and a mark, not only by color.
    • Text size iPhone and iPad: Vicu follows Dynamic Type.

    #Settings that always sync

    For quick reference, these follow your account to every device:

    • The Inbox project
    • Task order, global and per project, and subtask order
    • Custom lists and imported saved filters
    • Project categories and icons
    • Task templates
    • Plan default length and work hours
    • Your own Events
    • Saved place names and location reminders
    • Hidden shared routines and routine reminder choices
    • The date of your last weekly review
    • Pro's work area, cap and escalation rule, and Pro data

    Everything else stays on the device. Sync and offline explains how this works.
