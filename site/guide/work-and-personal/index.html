    Work and Personal

    One account can hold your job and the rest of your life. Tell Vicu once what is work, and a switch at the top of the sidebar shows work, everything else, or all of it.

    • Adding tasks in Work
    • Pro

    #In short

    • It is off until you turn it on, in Settings, General, Work and Personal. Off, Vicu is exactly as before.
    • You say what is work in two ways: a work label, and the projects that are work.
    • The switch has three positions. Work shows what is work. Personal shows everything else. All shows everything.
    • It is for focus, not for privacy. Search finds everything and reminders always come.
    • Each device has its own position. Your work computer can stay in Work while your phone stays in Personal. What counts as work is the same on all of them.

    #Set it up

    1. Open Settings, General, Work and Personal and turn on Show the Work and Personal switch.
    2. Choose a Work label. Any label will do; many people make one called "work".
    3. Under Work projects, tick the projects that are work.

    A ticked project takes its subprojects with it. To keep one subproject out, untick it; everything below it follows. You can also tick or untick a project from its menu: Work project.

    Note

    The Inbox cannot be a work project. It holds both kinds, and the work label tells them apart.

    #What is work

    A task is work when it is in a work project, or when it carries the work label. Every other task is personal. Nothing is stored on the task itself, so moving a task into a work project, or putting the label on it, makes it work on all your devices at once.

    Routines, your own Events and connected calendars belong to neither side. They show in every position.

    #What the switch changes

    Follows the switch
    Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Anytime, LogbookYes
    Label and project pages, custom listsYes
    The project list in the sidebarYes: Work shows the work projects, Personal the others
    Counts, the app badge, Quick ViewYes
    Review, guided sessions, the weekly reviewYes
    The task list beside the Plan timelineYes
    Search and the command paletteNo: they find everything, and mark what is on the other side
    Reminders and notificationsNo: they always come
    Routines, Events, calendarsNo

    A few details:

    • Subtasks stay with their task. A task shown in a list keeps all of its subtasks under it.
    • Time blocks stay on the timeline. On the Plan screen, a block from the other side stays where it is, drawn fainter, and counts as busy time. Nothing gets planned over a dentist's appointment because you were in Work.
    • A page you open on purpose tells you what it leaves out. A project or label page reached through search says "3 more in Personal" with Show.
    • Empty is not hidden. A project or category that holds nothing marked as work is simply Personal.

    #Adding tasks in Work

    A task you add while in Work should not vanish from the list you added it to. So in Work, a new task that is not in a work project gets the work label by itself. That covers the Inbox, quick add, Quick Entry, voice, pasted lines and templates.

    In Personal and All nothing is added, and Vicu never takes the label off or moves a task for you.

    Careful

    Without a work label, Vicu has nothing to add. A task you add in Work outside a work project then stays Personal, and Vicu says so with a way to open it. Choose a work label to avoid that.

    #Pro

    Two options tie Pro to this:

    • Show Pro only in Work (Settings, General, Work and Personal). In Personal the device behaves as it does with Pro off. Nothing of Pro is changed or lost, and it is all back when you switch to Work or All.
    • Work area: Work projects (Settings, Pro). Promises, people and the portfolio then apply to your work projects, so you keep one definition of work instead of two.

    #On your other devices

    The work label and the work projects are shared with your other devices through your account. The switch, its position and Show Pro only in Work are set on each device. The phone app has the same switch at the top of its menu, and Vicu in a browser has all of it.
