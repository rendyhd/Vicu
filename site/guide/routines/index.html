    Routines

    A routine is a habit with times of day. You tick it off, skip it or leave it, and it is never overdue. Use routines for what you do regularly and tasks for what you do once.

    #In short

    • A routine is not a repeating task. It has a name, a schedule and up to five times a day. A missed day is just "Not logged": routines never turn overdue and never pile up.
    • Open Routines from the sidebar (desktop) or the drawer (phone). Today's routines also show on Today.
    • Tick, skip or undo a routine at each of its times. Vicu keeps the history and shows how often you logged it.
    • Share a routine with someone you already share a project with. Whoever does it first ticks it for both.
    • Routines are optional. Turn them off in Settings and the sidebar entry, the Today section and the reminders go. Your routines and history are kept.

    #Make a routine All devices

    1. Open Routines and choose New routine.
    2. Type a Name, for example "Take vitamins".
    3. Pick a Color (desktop) or a colour from the picker (phone).
    4. Set the Schedule.
    5. Under Times & reminders, set each time of day.
    6. Choose Save.

    #Schedule

    ChoiceWhat it does
    On a schedulePick the weekdays, or Daily for every day. Set Repeat to every week, every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
    After completionShow again a number of days after you complete it. Use this for things like "every 2 weeks since the last time".

    #Times and reminders

    A routine has one to five times a day. Each time has:

    • A time of day
    • Remind me, which sends a notification at that time
    • Follow up (desktop), a second reminder 15 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour later. Off by default.

    Use Add time for more times. For example, "Feed the cat" at 08:00 and 18:00.

    Note

    A routine's colour is stored with the routine. Colours chosen on the desktop appear as a swatch on the phone.

    #Tick, skip and undo All devices

    Each occurrence has a status: done, skipped or not logged.

    • Tick the circle to mark a time done. Tick again to undo.
    • Skip leaves it out without counting as done. On the desktop, Skip shows when you hover the row. Undo takes a skip back.
    • A time you did nothing about becomes Not logged. It is not overdue and nothing nags.

    On the phone, the routines widget lets you log a routine without opening the app. On an iPhone, reminders for a routine have Complete and Skip actions.

    #Routines on Today All devices

    Today shows a Routines section with the routines still open for the day. Finished and skipped ones leave. The section folds from its header.

    Today shows one row per routine, at its next time, with progress like "1/3" when it has more than one time. To keep Today short, only routines due within two hours (or carried over from earlier) show at once. The rest wait behind one line such as "2 later today", or "Show 3 more", which opens until you leave the view.

    Set how many show:

    • Desktop: Settings, General, Routines, then Routines shown on Today: 1, 2 (the default), 3, 5 or All.
    • Phone: Settings, General, Routines.

    The same place has Enable routines and Show routines in Today. Turning off Show routines in Today keeps the Routines list but removes the section from Today. This is per device.

    #History and export All devices

    On the desktop, click a routine's name or its History button. It shows the share of logged times you completed ("Logged adherence"), the number of completions and every check-in with its date, time and status.

    Vicu keeps the last 400 days of detail with the routine. Older history is stored in hidden "Vicu routine archive" tasks on your server, and is still included in the history and in the export. Do not delete those tasks in the Vikunja web app.

    • Desktop: the Export button at the top of Routines saves all routines' history as a CSV file.
    • Phone: use Copy history as CSV in the Routines menu.

    #Reminders All devices

    Reminders follow each time's Remind me switch and the usual notification settings. See Settings for the notification options.

    • On the phone, Pause all reminders in the Routines menu silences every routine at once.
    • On an iPhone, routine reminders have Complete and Skip actions.
    • On Android, Vicu checks the routine on your server before a reminder rings, so a time already done elsewhere stays quiet.

    #Share a routine All devices

    You can share a routine with another person so that either of you ticking it counts for both, on every device. A routine is shared through a project you already share with that person. Vicu never creates or shares a project for you.

    1. Open the routine and find Share with.
    2. Pick a person. The list holds people from projects you share. People who can only view are listed but cannot be picked.
    3. If several projects fit, choose which one it moves into under Keep in.
    4. Save.

    The routine moves into that project. To stop, choose Only you under Share with; the routine goes back to your Inbox.

    #Who can do what

    OwnerMember who can editMember who can only view
    Tick, skip and undoYesYesNo
    EditYesYesNo
    Turn their own reminders off, hide itYesYesHide only
    Stop sharing, archive, deleteYesNoNo
    • A finished time says who did it, such as "Done by Maya" or "Done by you". If you both tick at the same moment, both count.
    • A member can turn off their own reminders for a shared routine, or hide it for themselves. Both follow them to their other devices. Hidden routines are listed under Hidden shared routines in Settings, General, Routines, where you can show them again.
    • Sharing needs a connection.
    • Deleting or archiving a project that holds a shared routine warns you first, even when you have turned off confirmations for deleting. Deleting the project deletes the routine and its history for everyone.

    #Archive a routine All devices

    Archive a routine you no longer do but want to keep. On the desktop, hover its row and choose the archive button. Archived routines wait under Archived at the bottom of the list, where the owner can Restore or delete one. Only a routine's owner archives or deletes it.
