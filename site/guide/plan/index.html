    Plan your day

    A time block says when you will do a task. The Plan screen shows your blocks on a timeline, and Plan my day builds a day for you to accept or change.

    #In short

    • A time block is a task with a start and an end time. Type Call Sam tomorrow at 3pm for 30m anywhere you add a task. Blocks work on every device, even with the Plan screen off.
    • The Plan screen is optional. It is off until you turn it on, per device. It shows a day timeline where you move, stretch, draw and drop blocks.
    • The due date still says which day a task belongs to. The block adds what time you set aside for it.
    • Plan my day is a four-step session that takes leftovers from yesterday, lets you pick what to do, arranges it in your free time and writes it only when you accept.
    • Nothing here needs the assistant. With the assistant set up, Plan my day can also suggest lengths and an order.

    Note

    Plan's settings sit with Vicu Pro's tools, but Plan does not need Pro: it works the same whether Pro is on or off. With Pro's Power tools on, Plan my day also uses your estimates and proposes a focus block.

    #Time blocks All devices

    A block is the task's own start and end time. Vikunja stores it as the task's start date and end date, so it shows in the Vikunja web app and on every device. A task without a block is a complete task, and nothing moves on its own.

    #Make a block

    Write the length after a time when you add a task, in the new-task row, in Quick Entry, in pasted lines, by voice or in the phone's quick add:

    • Call Sam tomorrow at 3pm for 30m
    • Write report friday at 9am for 2 hours

    You need a time of day. for 30m on its own stays in the title. More about this in Quick add.

    #What a block does

    • A task whose block starts today is on Today, even if its due date is later.
    • The row shows the block, for example "15:00 to 15:30". A date-only due date on the same day is left out, because the block already says the day.
    • Change the due date and a block on that day moves with it. Clear the due date and the time goes too.
    • A copy of a task keeps its block.
    • A default reminder, if you set one, goes by the block's start.

    Note

    Local tasks in standalone mode on the desktop have only a due date, so they have no time blocks.

    #Turn on the Plan screen All devices

    1. On the desktop, open Settings, Pro, Plan and turn on Show Plan in the sidebar. On the phone, open Settings, General and find Plan right after Pro.
    2. Open Plan from the sidebar (desktop) or the menu or bottom bar (phone). On the phone, Plan can take a slot in the bottom bar.

    The setting is per device. The things you set in the gear (default length, work hours) follow you to your other devices.

    #Use the timeline Desktop

    The Plan screen has a switch for Day, 3 days and Week. The week starts on Monday. The list of tasks sits on the left and the timeline on the right.

    #Place a task on a time

    • Drag a task from the list onto a time. It takes the default length: 30 minutes to start, changeable in the gear.
    • If the task is due before that time, its due date moves to that day and a message says so, with Undo. A later due date stays as a deadline.
    • Fit today in the gear gives the day's tasks without a time the next free slots, most urgent first, with Undo.

    #Move, resize and draw

    To do thisDo this
    Move a blockDrag it. It moves in 15-minute steps.
    Change the start or the endDrag the top or bottom edge.
    Set an exact lengthClick the block and pick a length.
    Add a new taskDrag on an empty time. Type a title. It goes into the project you are browsing, or the Inbox.
    Add a task at the next free timePress Ctrl+N (Cmd on a Mac).
    Remove the timeDrag the block back onto the list. The date stays.
    Move it by keyboardAlt+Shift+Up or Down moves a focused block 15 minutes. Shift+Up or Down changes its length. Enter opens the lengths.

    Pressing Escape or leaving the window while you drag cancels the change. The block is written when you let go.

    #Move a block to another day

    Click a block and use its day row: Today, Tomorrow, the next few days, or Pick a date. The block keeps its times, the view goes to that day, and there is an Undo. Past days cannot be picked.

    In Day view you can also drag a block or a task onto Back or Forward and hold it there for half a second to turn to the next day. Keep holding to go further, then drop on a time. Back stops at today.

    #The row above the timeline

    Tasks due on a day without a time sit in a row above that day's timeline. While you drag a block, the row shows "Drop here to remove the time".

    #The left panel

    Chips choose where the list takes its tasks from. Each shows a count once its list is loaded.

    • The selected day without a time ("Today")
    • Inbox
    • Next 7 days
    • No date
    • Project, which searches every project

    The box under the chips filters by title.

    #Routines and due times

    Routines show as small pills at their times. They have no length and never count as busy. Turn them off with Show routines on the timeline in the gear. Tasks with a due time also get a small marker on the timeline.

    #The gear

    The gear (Plan settings) holds the default length, your work hours (08:00 to 18:00 to start), Ask me to plan tomorrow, Show routines on the timeline and your calendars (see Events and calendars). Work hours and default length sync to your other devices. Free time is your work hours less what is already planned.

    #Use the timeline Phone

    On the phone

    The phone shows one day at a time, with a strip of seven days above it.

    • Swipe the timeline sideways for the next or previous day. Past the end of the strip, the strip moves a week. A swipe on the strip itself moves a week.
    • No time is the row above the hours. It holds the day's tasks without a time. Tap one and then a time to place it, press and hold to drag it, or tick it.
    • The tab at the right edge opens the tasks to plan, with the same chips as the desktop. Tap a task and then a time, or press and hold and drag it onto the timeline.
    • Press and hold a block to move it. Drag its handle to change the length. Both go in 15-minute steps.
    • Tap a block for another day, exact lengths and Remove time.
    • Another day while you drag: rest the block on a day of the strip for half a second, or tap the day with a second finger. Let go on the strip day to keep the same time on that day. Let go on No time to remove the time.
    • Press and hold an empty time to add a task or an event.
    • Today has a List and a Day switch while the planner is on. Day shows the timeline and lets you open and tick tasks. Changing them happens on Plan.
    • Under the days of the strip, small load bars show how full each day is.

    The gear has the default length, work hours, the evening nudge and the routine pills. It also lists your events and has New event.

    #Plan my day All devices

    Plan my day is a session for today or tomorrow. It writes nothing until you accept. On the desktop it opens from the Plan screen (the Plan my day button). On the phone it is the sun button on Plan.

    1. Leftovers. Unfinished tasks from before show one at a time. Choose Today, Tomorrow, No date or Done, or skip. Each choice is written at once.
    2. Choose. Tick what you will do, from the day, the Inbox, what is coming up or any project. A meter compares it with your free time. Click or tap a length to change it.
    3. Arrange. Dashed blocks show a draft in your free slots. Drag, stretch or keep a task without a time. On the phone, tap a block to move it earlier or later, change its length or keep it without a time. Accept the plan writes it.
    4. Ready. A summary of the day.

    On the last step:

    • Undo all puts back everything the session changed.
    • Remind me when each block starts adds a reminder to each block.
    • Ask me to plan tomorrow sends one notification each evening at a time you set, on this device. Tapping it opens Plan. You can also set it in the gear or in Settings, Pro, Plan.

    Events and connected calendars count as busy time, so the draft keeps clear of your meetings. See Events and calendars.

    #With the assistant

    With the assistant set up (see The assistant), a switch in the session adds suggestions. Each is dashed with a short reason, and nothing is saved until you accept.

    • Leftovers: one of the four choices for each, with Apply the suggestions.
    • Choose: how long each task takes, tasks worth adding, and what can wait when you are over your free time.
    • Arrange: an order with start times, long work in long gaps and quick tasks in short ones. First free slots is one tap away.

    It sends one request when the session opens, and another when you change the day or press Ask again. The request holds each task's title, project, priority, due date, subtask count and the start of its notes, plus your free time and your routines' times without their names. If it fails, the session says so once and carries on with first free slots and your default length. The assistant never sees event titles, only the free time.

    Pro

    With Pro on, estimates become the lengths, and the session can propose a daily focus block for tasks of your top initiatives.

    #Details

    • Where times show. Blocks show everywhere. Events and calendars show only on Plan and in Plan my day, not on Today, in lists or in reminders.
    • Past and busy. Timed, busy events count as busy for free time, Fit, the phone's load bars and Plan my day. All-day and free events do not.
