    90-day plans

    A 90-day plan is a project with a start and an end. Its tasks are the plan's items, their subtasks are the milestones, and the view shows week by week what was due, delivered, moved and dropped. Nobody fills in a status: the view counts what happened.

    #In short

    • A plan is a project. You give it a start day and an end day; 13 weeks unless you change it.
    • Items are the project's tasks. Milestones are an item's subtasks; an item without subtasks is its own milestone.
    • A milestone is a promise like any other: it has one owner and a date, and you give it a verdict when the date comes.
    • The view has a row per item and a column per week. A cell shows what happened in that week: how many milestones were due, delivered, delivered late, moved to a new date, or dropped.
    • Closing a plan can carry the open work into the next one, with its history.

    Pro

    This page is part of Vicu Pro and builds on Promises and people. Turn on Pro and the part Promises and people in Settings, Pro.

    #Make a project a 90-day plan Desktop

    1. Open the project's menu and choose 90-day plan.
    2. Set the First day. The Last day follows 13 weeks later; change it if your plan is shorter or longer.
    3. Choose a Group if the plan belongs to one, such as your leadership team. The plan is then listed on that group's page, and milestones you add in the plan get the group's label, so they are the group's actions too.
    4. Choose Save.

    The project stays a project: its tasks, sections and order are as they were. 90-day plan in the project's menu, and the link at the top of the project's page, open the plan's view.

    #Add items and milestones Desktop

    • Add item under the table adds a task to the project.
    • Open an item's row and use Add milestone to add a subtask. Type it like any promise:
    You typeYou get
    $Jan shortlist three regions fridayJan's milestone, due Friday
    Sign the contract 15 janA milestone without an owner yet

    You can also add subtasks to a task anywhere else in Vicu; they are the same milestones.

    An item shows its owner when it has one of its own, or when all its milestones have the same owner.

    #Read the view Desktop Phone

    Above the table: the plan's days, which week it is ("Week 4 of 13"), and how many milestones were delivered out of all that count.

    Each row is an item: its title, its owner, "2 of 4" delivered, and the next open milestone with its date. Each column is a week of the plan, counted from its first day. A cell says what happened in that week:

    • Due: milestones whose date is in that week.
    • Delivered, and how many of those were late.
    • Date moved: each time a milestone's date was moved with Not yet or New date.
    • Dropped: milestones that were dropped. A dropped milestone no longer counts in "2 of 4".

    The marks are shapes with a number: a circle for due, a check for delivered, a clock for delivered late, an arrow for a date that moved, a cross for dropped. Rest the pointer on a cell to read it in words. This week's column has a heavier edge.

    The view never gives a colour or a word such as "on track". It shows what happened and leaves the reading to you.

    #Give verdicts Desktop Phone

    A milestone with an owner asks for a verdict when its date comes, on Today and in Follow-ups, the same as any promise. Delivered, Late and Dropped complete it; Not yet and New date move it. A milestone with the group's label is also one of the group's actions, so it comes up in the group's session.

    A milestone without an owner counts as delivered when its task is ticked.

    #Close a plan and start the next Desktop

    1. In the plan's view choose Close plan.
    2. Leave Carry the open work into a new plan ticked to continue, and check the new project's name.
    3. Choose Close plan.

    The items that are not done, with their open milestones, move to a new project next to the old one. It is a plan of 13 weeks that starts the day after the old one ended. The tasks themselves move, so their promises and their history go with them. What was delivered stays in the closed plan, which links to the plan that carries on. Nothing more can be added to a closed plan.

    Carrying over needs a connection to your server. Without it, nothing is closed and nothing moves.

    #Details

    • Where it is stored. The plan's days are one of your synced settings, so the plan shows on your other devices. Nothing about progress is stored: every device counts it from the tasks and the verdicts.
    • Weeks are counted from the plan's first day, whatever day your weeks start on.
    • Subtasks of a milestone are not counted.
    • On the phone. Phone The phone shows a plan from Plan in the project's menu and from Plans on the group's page: a card per item with its weeks. Plans are made, changed and closed on the desktop or the web.
    • Without Pro, and on a version from before plans, the project is an ordinary project.
    • Removing the plan (in the plan's window) takes the days off. The project and its tasks stay.
