    Portfolio

    When you run several initiatives, the hard part is saying "not now". The Portfolio keeps the active ones few and ranked, and gives you the words for the conversation with whoever sponsors them.

    #In short

    • An initiative is a project you are steering, with a sponsor and an owner. You make one from the project's menu.
    • The cap limits how many initiatives are active at once: four unless you agree on another number. To start a new one at the cap, you park one first, with a reason.
    • Readiness is a nine-point check on each initiative. Adding a task to one that is not ready asks first.
    • Escalations are drafted when a promise is missed twice; the Sponsor desk gathers them with the decisions you are owed and a one-pager.
    • Requests (req:) go through triage: Accept, Delegate, Decline or Ask the sponsor.

    Pro

    This page is part of Vicu Pro and works on the desktop. Turn on Pro and the part Portfolio in Settings, Pro. Desktop

    #Make a project an initiative Desktop

    1. Open the project's menu (the ... button in the sidebar, or right-click the project).
    2. Choose Initiative, then Make this an initiative.

    The project now has an initiative panel at the top of its page, and it is listed on Portfolio in the sidebar. The same menu later offers Park (or Make active), Close (done) and Not an initiative, which turns it back into a plain project.

    On the initiative panel:

    • Sponsor and Owner: the people who asked for it and who runs it (people are labels you marked as a person; see Promises and people). Nobody yet until you choose.
    • Ready: how many of the nine readiness points are filled in. Open it to fill them in.
    • The log: note a Decision, a Value (a result it delivered) or an Open question as it happens. These feed the Sponsor desk, the weekly review and the quarterly impact.

    #Keep the active ones few Desktop

    Portfolio in the sidebar lists your initiatives:

    • Active, in rank order. Use the arrows (Move up, Move down) to rank them: the top ones are where your time goes first. Each shows its owner and how ready it is (for example Ready 6/9). A bar at the top shows how many of the cap's places are taken.
    • Parked, with the reason each was parked.
    • Closed.

    The cap is four active initiatives. When you are at it, Portfolio says "At the cap: park one before starting another", and making another initiative active asks you to Choose one to park first. That is the point: something has to give, and Vicu makes it a decision instead of a drift.

    To change the cap, open Settings, Pro, Portfolio: set Active initiatives at most, and record who you Agreed with and when. A cap is only useful when the person who sponsors your work agreed to it.

    #Park an initiative

    1. On Portfolio, click the park button on the initiative, or choose Initiative, Park in the project's menu.
    2. Give a reason, for example "waiting for next quarter's budget".
    3. Choose Park.

    "Its tasks wait until it is active again. The reason is kept with it." While parked, its tasks leave Today and Upcoming and show as On hold in your lists, on the desktop and on the phone. Choose Make active to bring it back.

    #Check readiness Desktop

    An initiative that starts without the basics tends to stall. The readiness check has nine points:

    PointThe question it answers
    SponsorWho asked for this and can decide on it?
    OwnerWho runs it day to day?
    Why nowWhy this, and why now?
    Done looks likeHow will we know it is finished?
    Smallest useful stepWhat is the smallest result that is already useful?
    Not doingWhat is deliberately out of scope?
    Who decides whatWhich decisions are whose?
    Capacity agreedDid the sponsor agree to the time it takes? Use Mark agreed today
    Stop ifWhen do we stop?

    Adding a task to an initiative that is not ready asks "This initiative is not ready yet. Log the task anyway?" Choose Log anyway to add it; Vicu counts how often that happens, which is worth raising with the sponsor.

    #Deal with missed promises Desktop

    When someone misses the same promise twice, Vicu drafts an escalation: a short note for the sponsor that names the promise and how often it was missed. When the same person also has an open promise in another initiative, the draft asks which comes first, A or B. Otherwise it asks whether the sponsor can help unblock it, or whether what was agreed should change. You set after how many misses it drafts one in Settings, Pro, Portfolio, Draft an escalation after this many misses, and record when you agreed on the rule with Escalation rule agreed on.

    Escalations wait on the Sponsor desk. Nothing is sent by Vicu.

    #Use the Sponsor desk Desktop

    Sponsor desk in the sidebar is your preparation for the next conversation with a sponsor:

    • Escalations: each draft with Put on the agenda, Copy, Resolved (note what was decided) and Dismiss. A decision you note is added to the log.
    • Decisions needed: questions you asked the sponsor, with how long they have been waiting. Choose Decided when you have an answer.
    • Capacity not agreed: initiatives whose sponsor has not agreed to the time yet.
    • Parked initiatives.

    The one-pager sums it up for the meeting: copy it into a document or mail, or print it.

    #Triage a request Desktop

    A request is something someone asks you to take on. Instead of adding it to your plate straight away, weigh it against the ranked portfolio.

    1. Type req: at the start of the title (req: Ana wants a dashboard for sales), or right-click an existing task and choose Treat as request.
    2. The triage window opens next to your ranked initiatives. Choose one:
      • Accept: Choose an initiative to put it in.
      • Delegate: say To whom. A "Confirm with" task reminds you to check they took it.
      • Decline: it is logged, so you can show what you said no to.
      • Ask the sponsor: it becomes a decision you are owed, on the Sponsor desk.
    3. Choose Do it and copy the reply. A short reply for the person who asked is on your clipboard.

    #Details

    • Turning this part off hides the panel, Portfolio and the Sponsor desk. Your initiatives, logs and escalations wait on your server. Parked initiatives stop hiding their tasks while the part is off.
    • On the phone. Phone The Portfolio screens are desktop only. The phone follows parked initiatives, so their tasks stay out of Today and Upcoming there too.
    • Looking back. The weekly review and the quarterly impact summary read the portfolio. See Power tools.
