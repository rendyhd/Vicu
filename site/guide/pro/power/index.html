    Guide / Vicu Pro

    Power tools

    Faster ways through a lot of tasks, and a regular look back. Each tool here works on its own; use the ones that fit how you work.

    #In short

    • Pro lists: custom lists can pick tasks by availability, flag, estimate, project status, promise and person, group them, and choose what each row shows. Four starter lists get you going.
    • The table: show a custom list as a table and sort by any column.
    • The inspector: set the start date, estimate and flag of many tasks at once.
    • Plan my day uses your estimates and proposes a focus block for your top initiatives.
    • The weekly review gets execution steps and a mirror line; Portfolio gets a quarterly impact summary.

    Pro

    This page is part of Vicu Pro and works on the desktop. Turn on Pro and the part Power tools in Settings, Pro. Desktop

    #Make a Pro list Desktop

    Custom lists are the place for views of your own (see Custom lists). With Power tools on, the list editor has a Pro section:

    SettingChoices
    ShowEvery task, Available now, Remaining (open)
    FlaggedAny, Flagged, Not flagged
    Estimate at most (minutes)Only tasks estimated at this length or less
    Project statusAny, Active, On hold
    PromiseAny task, Promised to me, I promised, Either
    PeopleOnly tasks for these people
    Group byNo groups, Project, Label, Person, Due date, Start date, Flagged
    Rows showWhich details each row shows: Project, Labels, Due date, Start date, Estimate, Flag, Notes

    The editor counts the open tasks that match as you change the settings.

    #Start from a ready-made list

    On a new list, Start from offers four lists you can use as they are or change:

    StarterWhat it shows
    Next actionsEvery available task, grouped by project
    Waiting onPromises others made you, grouped by person, by due date
    Quick winsAvailable tasks of 15 minutes or less, with project, due date and estimate
    Flagged by projectFlagged tasks, grouped by project

    Note

    A list's Pro conditions are kept and followed on every device, Pro on or off, and on the phone too. A list made for "Quick wins" never suddenly shows everything somewhere else. The editor keeps showing the Pro section for such a list even where Power tools are off, so you can change it.

    #See a list as a table Desktop

    At the top of a custom list, switch between Rows and Table.

    • Click a column's header to sort by it; click again to reverse.
    • Shift+click a second column to sort by it within the first.
    • Rows select like list rows: click, Ctrl+click and Shift+click (Cmd on a Mac), and open with a double click or Enter.

    #Change many tasks at once Desktop

    The inspector sets Pro's fields of several tasks in one go.

    1. Select two or more tasks.
    2. Press Ctrl+I, or choose Inspector in the selection bar.
    3. Set Start, Estimate or Flag (Flag all or Unflag). A field that differs between the tasks says Mixed; Clear removes it from all of them.

    #Plan my day with estimates and a focus block Desktop

    Plan my day lays your day out as time blocks. With Power tools on:

    • A task's estimate is its length in the plan, so the meter shows how full your day really is.
    • The plan starts with a focus block of 60 to 90 minutes "for the work that moves your top initiatives": the available tasks of your highest-ranked initiatives. Keep it, or untick its tasks like any other.

    #The weekly review, for execution Desktop

    The weekly review gets five steps before its usual ones, each with a count:

    StepWhat it is about
    Give verdictsPromises others made you that are due: kept, not yet, or another verdict
    Send what was agreedWhat was agreed in a meeting and not sent in a recap yet
    Escalations and decisionsEscalations drafted or on the agenda, and decisions you are owed
    Check the portfolioInitiatives not ready, over the cap or without an available task
    Note the value deliveredInitiatives closed this week without the value they delivered

    Each step has Open to go there and Mark done.

    The mirror is one line about your last seven days, for example: "Last 7 days: you took over 1 promise and had a focus block on 3 of 5 workdays. You hold 42 open tasks; others hold 6 promises to you." It is a reflection, not a score.

    #Look back on a quarter Desktop

    On Portfolio, choose Quarterly impact for a summary of This quarter or Last quarter: the value delivered, the initiatives closed, the decisions made and how promises went. Add a Growth note about your own quarter; it appears only in the copy. Copy it for a performance review or a report, or print it.
