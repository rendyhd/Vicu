    #Vicu Pro

    Vicu stays simple for everyone. Vicu Pro is an optional switch for more complex work: what you can do right now, promises people made you, initiatives you steer, and faster ways through many tasks. Pro only adds; with it off, Vicu is the app you know. New to it? Watch the five-minute tour.

    • What Pro isWho it is for, its four parts, and how to try it safely.
    • Know what you can do nowAvailable tasks, start dates, waiting on other tasks, flags.

    • PortfolioInitiatives, the cap, readiness, escalations and the Sponsor desk.
    • Power toolsPro lists, the table, the inspector and the weekly review.
    • Plan your dayTime blocks, the Plan screen and Plan my day. Works with Pro on or off.

    #About Vicu

    Vicu is a free, open-source task manager for Vikunja, the open-source to-do server you host yourself. Your tasks live on your server, not with Vicu. Vicu is an independent app, not made by the Vikunja team. Found something that is wrong or missing here? Each page has a link at the bottom to suggest a change, or open an issue.
